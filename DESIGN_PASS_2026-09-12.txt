FSU — interface tidy-up, 12 Sep 2026
Presentation only. No behaviour, wording or control changed. All edits are in src/app.css;
no render function or markup was touched. Built with `node build.js`.


WHAT THE PASS WAS FOR

Three things had accumulated: per-rule padding and font sizes (every component carried its
own 13px / 13.5px / 14px / 14.5px / 16.5px and its own 9px / 10px / 11px / 13px / 18px), a
lot of chrome per row (filled panel headers, cards nested inside cards, heavy badges), and
tap targets under the 44px guideline nearly everywhere at iPad width.


1. ONE SPACING SCALE, ONE TYPE SCALE

Added to :root in src/app.css, above the theme blocks. They carry no colour, so they need no
matching entry in the three theme overrides:

  --s1..--s6   4 8 12 16 24 32
  --f0..--f6   11 12.5 13.5 15 17 21 26
  --r1..--r3   10 14 20
  --tap        44

Every shared component is now sized from these instead of from its own number: rows, panels,
tiles, badges, headings, fields, and every bar of buttons. The odd sizes that were in the file
(13px, 14px, 14.5px, 15px, 16.5px, 19px, 22px, 24px, 26px, 28px, 30px) collapse onto seven
steps. Sketch-canvas SVG classes, the print sheet and the label sheet keep their own numbers —
those are drawing and paper geometry, not interface, and forcing them onto a screen scale would
be wrong.

A side effect worth naming: the header title had three competing `body.wide .hdr h1` rules
(30px, 26px, 24px) where only the last had any effect. The two dead rules are deleted and the
title is one size, --f6.


2. VIEW BY VIEW

HOME — the screen the brief singled out.
  · The four stat tiles now only carry colour when something is wrong. "Stock: Fine",
    "Expiring: None", "Gaps: None" were all printed in green, so three green tiles competed
    with the one amber tile that actually needed the technician. .stile.t-calm .sv is now
    --ink. The amber SWEEP tile is the only coloured thing above the fold.
  · Panel headers ("Scenes", "Next actions", "Units", "Quick find") were filled grey bars with
    a rule under them — a band of chrome per panel. They are now the same quiet caption as the
    `.sect` headings used everywhere else: same size, same weight, same colour, no fill, no
    rule. One heading treatment in the app instead of two.
  · The Units panel held three bordered, rounded bay cards inside a bordered, rounded panel —
    boxes in boxes. They are now flush rows separated by a hairline, matching the "Next actions"
    rows directly opposite them. The panel is the box; the units are rows in it.
  · The meta strip (van name, date, initials, sweep, vehicle check, Settings) is on the scale
    and is 44px tall, so "Set your initials" and the Settings button are properly tappable.
  · Tile weight 800 -> 700 and letter-spacing eased; 800 at 21px was shouting.

STORAGE / COMPARTMENTS
  · The bay card wasted its right half. The status badge was centred on a full-width second row
    while the van diagram sat in a 122px column on row one only, leaving a hole under it. The
    diagram now spans both rows and the badge sits left, under the text it describes. Card
    padding 18/20 -> 16, radius 20 -> 14, title 22px/800 -> 21px/700. Same information, about
    15px shorter and with no dead centre.
  · The four action buttons (Printable map, Printable labels, Scan a label, Vehicle check) were
    42px tall; they are 44 now and on the scale.

BAY
  · Inherits the bay-card change above and the .row change below. Untouched otherwise — the
    compartment grid is a plan of a physical wall and its container queries are load-bearing.

INVENTORY / ITEMS
  · The desktop table was 14px with 11px cells, giving 43px rows. Now 15px with --s3/--s4
    cells and a 1.45 line-height: 46px rows, readable across the seven columns, and past the
    tap guideline for the iPad.
  · Column headers went 11.5px -> 11px with slightly more tracking, so they read as labels
    rather than as small text.
  · The five grouping chips (Compartment, Category, ...) were 42px; 44 now.
  · The list-row form used on the narrower layouts: compartment code 16.5px -> 17px with a
    tightened line-height, description 13.5px -> --f2. Rows gained about 6px each. On a 62-row
    sweep list that is real scroll, and it is the one place I deliberately spent height rather
    than saved it — a gloved thumb is the reason the brief exists.

SWEEP
  · The next-compartment bar, the "Swept, next" / "Change" buttons, the Quick add / Full details
    switch and every field are all 44px now. The fields in particular were 41px.
  · Section headings ("Bin wall — 0 of 27 swept") on the scale.

INCIDENT / SCENES
  · The incident tile is the same family as the bay card and got the same treatment: padding,
    radius, title weight, and the plan badges moved from centred to left-aligned so they line up
    with the case number above them.
  · The document rows (Entry log, Evidence log, ...) are the shared .row, so they picked up the
    scale and the 44px minimum.

SETTINGS (data)
  · .setgroups is a two-column grid and its panels were stretching to the tallest in the row,
    so "You" was a 130px-tall white box containing one 52px row. align-items:start — the panels
    are now the height of their contents.

GUIDE, FORMS, TEMPLATES, FILL, ACTIVE, TIDY, REORDER
  · No structural change; they are built from the shared primitives (.row, .panel, .filters,
    .editbar, .chips, .fld, .btn, .back) and inherit the whole pass.

SKETCH
  · Only the shared controls: .polybar, .objbar, .zoombar and the symbol filter chips are 44px
    now. Canvas geometry, the symbol palette previews, the layer rail and the ink swatches are
    untouched — see "left alone".

PRINT / LABELS
  · Untouched. These render onto paper at fixed sizes.


3. WHAT I DELIBERATELY LEFT ALONE

  · The sketch canvas SVG classes (.k-*), the print sheet (.psheet, .pgrid, .ptab) and the
    label sheet. Paper and drawing geometry; a screen type scale does not belong there.
  · The ink swatches in the sketch rail (34x34) and the layer-row preview buttons. Taking them
    to 44 would push the tool rail past its column at 1194 and they are a precision control
    used with the same finger that is already on the canvas. They are the one family still
    under the guideline and I would rather flag them than break the rail.
  · The van illustration, the cargo-slot container queries and the bay-plan container queries.
    They are carefully tuned to degrade from a 104-inch shelf down to a 16px slot and they work.
  · The compartment grid density in Bay. Making those cells bigger would mean fewer on screen,
    which is the opposite of what a wall map is for.
  · The bottom tab bar (.tabs). It only appears below 1000px, which is outside the two targets.
  · Colour tokens. Every value in the four theme blocks is unchanged. The only colour edit in
    the pass is which token a calm tile's number uses.


4. THINGS I THINK SHOULD CHANGE BUT DID NOT, BECAUSE THEY ARE BEHAVIOUR

  · Home says the same thing twice. The SWEEP tile reads "62 left / 0 of 62 checked" and four
    rows below it "Next actions" reads "Sweep 62 compartments". Same for Stock/low, Expiring
    and Gaps: all four tiles have a matching row. Either the tiles or those four rows could go
    and the screen would lose nothing and gain a lot of calm. That is a content decision.
  · The view name is printed twice on every tab: once as the header <h1> and again as the bold
    word starting the meta strip ("Storage / 62 compartments in 3 bays / no sweep yet"). The
    meta strip could start with the facts.
  · "Settings" appears twice on home — the button at the end of the meta strip and the row at
    the bottom of the side bar. Both are wanted on a phone; on the two target machines one is
    redundant.
  · Two-column grids (Settings, and the home dashboard) leave a gap when one column's panel is
    much shorter. A masonry or column-flow layout fixes it but changes reading order, which
    changes where a technician expects to find things.
