FSU app

Van inventory and scene documentation for the Forensic Services Unit. Two icons, one set of records, and both work offline once installed.

FSU — the van https://drfllcky13-droid.github.io/fsu/

Storage, items, the sweep, the guide, labels and settings.

Scenes — the scene https://drfllcky13-droid.github.io/fsu/scenes.html

Incidents, the documents each one needs, and the sketch.

Install both. They are one app sharing one set of records: a sweep logged on one is there on the other, and the tabs down the side move between them.

Put both on the home screen

iPad or iPhoneOpen the address in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
AndroidOpen the address in Chrome. Tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
DesktopOpen the address in Chrome or Edge. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

Do it once for each address above, so both icons are on the home screen. Open each from its own icon once while online. After that they work with no connection, and update themselves the next time they are opened online. Then, still online, open Settings, This device, and tap Download for offline use: that fetches the map of Williamsport, its addresses and the PDF, Word and QR tools in one go (about 11 MB), so every part works at a scene with no signal.

Share the van list with the other devices

  1. On the FSU icon, open Settings (the gear at the top right on a phone, or Settings at the bottom of the side bar on an iPad), then Automatic saving.
  2. Owner and repository are already filled in. Paste the unit's access token and enter the date it expires.
  3. Tap Connect. The list pulls straight away, and every change after that saves itself a couple of seconds later.

Done once per device, on the FSU icon; the Scenes icon picks it up. The token is kept in that device's browser only. Ask the unit's GitHub administrator for the token; it is not printed here.

What stays on the device

Incidents, forms, sketches and photographs never sync. They leave the device only when you export a PDF, Word file, DXF or a case package.
