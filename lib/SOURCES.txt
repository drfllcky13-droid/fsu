MapLibre finds its worker as ./maplibre-gl-worker.mjs next to maplibre-gl.mjs, so the three .mjs
files must stay together. Their sourceMappingURL comments point at .map files that are not copied;
only a browser's developer tools ever ask for them.

jsPDF went from 2.5.1 to 4.2.1 on 2026-09-23 for the published advisories against 2.5.1 (the last,
GHSA-wfv2-pwc8-crg5 and GHSA-7x6v-j9x4-qf24, affect up to 4.2.0). svg2pdf went to 2.8.1 with it,
because 2.2.4 only works with jsPDF 2. Both files are identical to dist/ in the npm packages
jspdf@4.2.1 and svg2pdf.js@2.8.1. jsPDF's optional helpers (DOMPurify, html2canvas, canvg) are
not copied: they serve doc.html() and SVG-as-image, which the app does not use.
